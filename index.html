FAQ

What is Beer Views?
Beer Views is an event series where professors and experts give thought-provoking lectures inside of bars.
How do I buy tickets?
On our Eventbrite here ➡️ https://lecturesontap.com/cities
What happens at Beer Views?
Beer Views starts with about 20 minutes of getting checked in and getting snacks and drinks.

Then, there's a fun and interactive opener so come chatty. After that, the lecture speaker of the night will take the stage for about 40 minutes. We end with Q&A.

Typically, folks hang around for a bit afterwards to chat about their thoughts on the lecture.
Where do Beer Views events take place?
Beer Views events take place in various bars in London. You can see the neighborhood where an event will be located by looking at the event details.

⚠️ The exact address is given in the confirmation email you receive after you get tickets.
What if I don't drink?
Mocktails and/or bites to eat are available at every Beer Views event. Most of our bars also offer coffee, tea, and desserts as well.
I want to present at Beer Views. What do I do?
Thank you for your interest in presenting a lecture for Beer Views. Please submit your lecture idea here ➡️ https://airtable.com/appGaVHfPtEEtSbZO/pagaErpEs9MrIGl9q/form

What we're looking for are unique lecture ideas from folks with expertise. If you have an idea that you think would pair well over drinks, we'd love to hear from you!
Do I need to buy tickets?
Yes. We do not sell tickets at the door.
What if an event is sold out? Is there an email list?
Yes, we have an email list. We recommend getting on the list if you want to be notified when new events are added to the calendar. Here's the link ➡️ https://emaillist.lecturesontap.com/
